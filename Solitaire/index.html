📖 About the Game

Solitaire: Classic Card Puzzle brings the classic Klondike Solitaire card game to your mobile device with smooth animations, clear card faces, and intuitive controls. Whether you are taking a short break or relaxing at home, enjoy endless hours of pure card-stacking fun.

✨ Key Features

  • 🃏 Classic Klondike Rules: Play standard Klondike Solitaire with Draw 1 and Draw 3 modes.
  • 👆 Smooth Controls: Drag & Drop cards effortlessly or Double-Tap to auto-move cards to foundation slots.
  • 💡 Smart Hints & Undo: Get helpful visual hints when you are stuck and unlimited undo to test different strategies.
  • 🪄 Magic Wand: Instantly uncover hidden cards to keep the game flowing.
  • 🎉 Auto-Complete & Winning Animations: Automatically finish solved games with celebratory card cascade effects.
  • 📶 100% Offline Play: No Wi-Fi or internet connection required.
  • 🎨 Clean Casino Felt Design: Crisp card faces, large readable numbers, and gentle sound effects.

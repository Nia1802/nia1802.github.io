About the Game

Hexa Garden: Color Stacks is a color-sorting puzzle played on a hexagonal board. Place stacks of colorful chips in empty spaces and bring matching colors together. Build groups of the same color, clear chips toward the level's goals, and leave room for your next placement.

Features

  • Arrange colorful chip stacks on a hexagonal board
  • Match the colors at the tops of neighboring stacks
  • Build matching groups to clear chips
  • Set up chain reactions through thoughtful placements
  • Collect the colors needed to complete each level
  • Balance your color goals with the space left on the board

Plan Your Placements

Study the top color of each stack and the spaces around it. Think about how a match could uncover another color and create an opportunity for your next move.

Challenge Yourself

Meet each level's color goals before the board fills up. Choose your placements carefully and look for combinations that clear chips while keeping useful spaces open.

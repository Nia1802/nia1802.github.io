About the Game

Triple Tile: Match 3 Classic is a fruit tile puzzle from PineApple Studio. Select uncovered tiles and collect three of the same fruit to clear them. Plan your moves carefully: your tray has only seven slots, and each cleared layer reveals new choices.

Key Features

  • 100 levels: Explore increasingly challenging layered tile layouts.
  • Colorful fruit tiles: Match familiar fruits, including watermelon and pineapple, with more varieties introduced as you progress.
  • Helpful boosters: Use undo, shuffle, hints, and extra holding space when you need help.
  • Saved progress: Return to unlocked levels and track your completed puzzles.
  • A fresh fruit theme: Enjoy a bright background, clear tiles, and smooth animations.
  • Offline puzzles: Play levels without an internet connection. Ads and optional rewarded bonuses require internet access.

How to Play

  1. Tap a tile that is not covered by another tile to move it into the tray.
  2. Collect three identical fruit tiles to remove them from the tray.
  3. Clear all tiles to finish the level, while keeping the seven-slot tray from filling up.

Arrow Garden: Escape Puzzle

Tap each arrow out of the garden, but only when its path is clear.

View on Google Play

About the Game

Every arrow points one way and leaves the board only when nothing blocks its path to the edge. Find the order that clears every arrow. From level 5 each board has 3 hearts and a countdown, and a tap on a blocked arrow costs a heart.

Features

  • 150 puzzles that grow from 5 × 5 to 8 × 8 stone gardens
  • 3 hearts and a timer on every board from level 5
  • Daily Challenge with 8 to 12 boards and a reward
  • Hint and Undo helpers, with a free gift every day
  • Every board can be solved without watching an ad

Download on Google Play

About the Game

Color Lab: Sort & Reveal is a liquid-sorting puzzle that turns a mix of colors into neatly sorted tubes. Tap a tube to select it, then tap another to pour. You can pour into an empty tube or onto the same top color, as long as there is room. Complete the puzzle by filling each non-empty tube with a single color.

Features

  • Sort layered colors with simple tap-to-pour controls
  • Plan each pour around the space available in the tubes
  • Undo moves or restart a puzzle to try another approach
  • Use hints when you need help finding the next move
  • Reveal postcard artwork as you complete levels
  • Return for a daily sorting challenge

Think a Few Pours Ahead

Empty tubes give you room to rearrange the colors. Look beneath the top layer, keep space available, and plan how each pour will help you complete a full tube.

Challenge Yourself

Turn mixed layers into tubes of a single color, then take on the next puzzle. Try the daily challenge and continue revealing artwork in your gallery.

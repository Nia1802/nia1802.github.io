Unscrew the bolts, drop the plates, and pack every screw in its color box.

View on Google Play

About the Game

Colorful screws hold stacked plates to the board. Tap a screw to send it to the box of its color; if no box matches yet, it waits in the tray. Plates on top cover the screws below, so choose your order carefully and drop every plate to clear the level.

Features

  • 100 puzzles that get harder as you go
  • Every level can be solved without watching an ad
  • Color marks on every screw for easier color recognition
  • Saved progress and replayable unlocked levels

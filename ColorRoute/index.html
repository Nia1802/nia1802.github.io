About the Game

Worker ants carry nectar along garden trails. Tap the green leaves to turn their arrows, then press GO ANTS! and all ants march together, one step at a time. Each ant must reach the anthill of its own color without bumping into another ant. If a run fails, the board returns to your route so you can adjust it and try again.

Features

  • 100 garden puzzles across 4 chapters
  • All ants move at the same time, so timing and crossings matter
  • No timer and no lives: adjust your route and run again
  • Saved progress and replayable unlocked levels

About the Game

Choose a postcard and drag pieces from the tray onto the board. Close to its spot, a piece clicks into place and stays there; pieces dropped elsewhere wait on the board until you need them. There is no timer and no penalty for trying.

Features

  • 24 original postcards in four sets
  • 72 puzzles at 16, 36 and 64 pieces
  • Daily Postcard with 36 pieces
  • Faint picture guide you can switch on or off for free
  • Every piece stays where you left it when you come back

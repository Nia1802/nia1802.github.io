About the Game

Fruit Drop: Orchard Merge is a fruit-merging puzzle with simple controls and playful physics. Choose where to drop each fruit into the container. When two fruits of the same type touch, they merge into a larger fruit and add to your score. Keep combining fruits to reach the watermelon while keeping the pile below the danger line.

Features

  • Drop fruits into the container with a simple tap
  • Merge matching pairs to discover larger fruits
  • Watch fruits bounce, roll, and settle into place
  • Create chain reactions with carefully placed drops
  • Work your way from small fruits to a watermelon
  • Challenge yourself to beat your personal best

Make Every Drop Count

Look for matching pairs and consider where each fruit will land. A well-placed drop can trigger several merges, free up space, and set up your next move.

Challenge Yourself

How far can you climb the fruit chain? Keep the pile under control, reach the watermelon, and see how high you can push your score before the container fills up.

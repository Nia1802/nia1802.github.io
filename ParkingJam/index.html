Parking Jam: Car Out Escape

Tap cars in the right order to clear 100 parking puzzles.

View on Google Play

About the Game

The parking lot is packed. Each car follows the arrow shown on it. Tap a car when its path to the exit is clear; if another car blocks the way, move the others first. Clear the whole lot to finish the level.

Features

  • 100 solvable parking puzzles with increasing challenge
  • Simple one-tap controls and clear directional arrows
  • No timer or move limit
  • Saved progress and replayable unlocked levels

Plan Each Escape

Look for an open path, then decide which car should leave first. A blocked car stays in place, so the order of your taps makes all the difference.
